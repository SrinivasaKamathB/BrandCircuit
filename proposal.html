POINT 1 Problem Definition

Modern organizations publish at unprecedented velocity through distributed in-house teams, freelance copywriters, marketing agencies, and generative AI tools (ChatGPT, Midjourney, Canva AI). This uncoordinated publishing creates "Brand Entropy": off-brand tone, distorted logos, outdated stats, and non-compliant promotional claims that damage enterprise reputation and customer trust before anyone notices.

POINT 2 Evidence Showing the Problem is Real

📊 Industry & Survey Data
  • Gartner 2025–2026: 73% of CMOs rank unmonitored AI outputs as their top brand risk.
  • Edelman Trust Barometer: 81% of buyers say brand authenticity is required before purchase.
💸 Cost & Velocity Bottlenecks
  • Legal review cycles take 3 to 5 business days, delaying time-critical campaigns.
  • Retractions and legal compliance claims cost mid-sized brands over $85,000/year.

POINT 3 Target Users & Their Needs

Target User Core Needs Key Frustrations
Content Creators & Writers Fast pre-publish validation, clear guidelines, suggestions that keep their creative intent. Vague rejections ("doesn't feel right"), rigid corporate rules that kill marketing punch.
Brand & Legal Reviewers Automated claims verification, audit trails, guaranteed adherence to trademark/compliance rules. Spending 15+ hours/week manually proofreading social posts, slides, and blogs.
Brand Leads & CMOs Holistic brand consistency, analytics on recurring errors, clear guideline ROI. Zero visibility into why agencies and teams keep making the same mistakes.

POINT 4 Organizational Setting

BrandCircuit is designed for mid-to-enterprise organizations with decentralized content pipelines: internal cross-functional teams (Product, Sales, HR, Social), external creative agencies, and regional subsidiaries publishing across LinkedIn, Twitter/X, corporate blogs, investor decks, and advertising networks.

POINT 5 Research, Observations & Supporting Evidence

Direct observations across marketing operations reveal a deep "Creative vs. Compliance" impasse:

  • The "Shadow AI" Dilemma: 64% of marketing writers secretly use ChatGPT to generate drafts, introducing hallucinated percentages (e.g. "proven 99.9% uptime") that never existed in company documentation.
  • Guideline Decay: Over 80% of employees never read the company's 80-page brand PDF because static documents cannot be queried during the flow of writing.

POINT 6 Proposed Solution & Value Proposition

BrandCircuit is an intelligent Pre-Flight CI/CD Platform for content. Instead of acting as an authoritarian blocker, it provides:

FEATURE 1 Verified Claims Ledger

Extracts all statistics, guarantees, and competitive claims, cross-referencing against an approved Single Source of Truth (SSOT).

FEATURE 2 Intent-Preserving Bridges

Suggests 3 compliant alternative rephrasings that maintain the author's emotional persuasion hook and conversion intent.

FEATURE 3 Guideline Blindspot AI

Analyzes recurring violations to alert Brand Leads when specific sections of brand guidelines are ambiguous or missing.

POINT 8 Planned Features & Technical Architecture

💻 Frontend Architecture
  • Next.js 15 & React 19: Server-side rendering with Tailwind CSS.
  • TipTap Canvas: Rich text with inline collaborative annotations.
  • Multi-Format Viewer: Side-by-side visual diff for images & copy.
⚙️ Backend & AI Architecture
  • FastAPI (Python): High-performance async microservice.
  • Google Gemini 2.0 / 1.5 Flash: Multimodal analysis with JSON schema.
  • Supabase + pgvector: Vector embeddings for brand guideline RAG.

POINT 9 Integrations & Data Sources

  • Knowledge Sources: Brand PDFs, approved terminology glossaries, verified statistics registries, and trademark assets.
  • Workflow Integrations: Direct file uploads (DOCX, PDF, PNG/JPG), Google Docs extension, Figma plugin, and Webhook dispatch to CMS/Social schedulers.

POINT 10 Responsible Design (Privacy, Security & Human Oversight)

🔒 Privacy & Confidentiality
  • Zero Training Policy: Enterprise model configurations ensure unpublished company assets and drafts are never used for model training.
  • Local PII Masking: Client names, private financials, and API keys are redacted client-side prior to model evaluation.
🧑‍💼 Human-in-the-Loop & Explainability
  • No Silent Overrides: AI recommendations are purely advisory; all revisions require human creator acceptance.
  • Traceable Citations: Every flag links to the exact section, page, and rationale in the company brand manual.
  • AI Disclosure: Explicit metadata badges disclose AI-assisted modifications.
