3 Technology & Implementation Details

💻 Frontend Architecture
  • Responsive Cyber-Themed UI: High-contrast dark aesthetic with Tailwind CSS, custom glowing badges, and Lucide/FontAwesome iconography.
  • Dynamic Policy Engine: Active text scanner that analyzes custom copy for prohibited guarantees, unverified claims, and aggressive tone.
  • HTML5 Canvas Visual Pipeline: Interactive image inspector rendering logo boundaries and color drift analysis.
⚙️ Backend & Security Architecture
  • Node.js v24 Microserver (server.js): Lightweight, zero-dependency async HTTP server ensuring 100% portability.
  • Real WebCrypto API Cryptography: Mathematically valid SHA-256 content hashing for tamper detection.
  • Enterprise Hardening: Production OWASP headers including strict CSP, X-Frame-Options: DENY, nosniff, and zero data retention.

4 Explanation of Changes Made to Original Plan

During Round 1, our conceptual proposal focused on static text highlighting and standard handbook lookups. In building the Round 2 MVP, we implemented three deliberate architectural evolutions:

  • From Passive Warnings to "PR Blast Radius": Simple red warning banners fail to motivate marketing teams. We introduced the PR Blast Radius & Crisis Simulator to visualize the concrete cost of failure (viral backlash and FTC regulatory exposure), creating an immediate executive business case.
  • From Rigid LLM Overrides to "Multi-Agent Negotiation": Copywriters reject standard AI rewrites because they remove creative voice and conversion punch. We introduced the Maverick vs. Guardian Debate with a 3-tier compromise slider, enabling creators to balance legal safety with growth hooks.
  • From Status Flags to "Cryptographic Passports": Rather than a superficial "Approved" checkbox, we engineered a verifiable SHA-256 digital passport to prevent post-approval content tampering in enterprise publishing pipelines.

5 Three Major Improvements for Round 3 (The Mastermind Roadmap)

As mandated by competition rules, these three meaningful additions represent deep architectural expansions:

IMPROVEMENT 1 Self-Healing Playbook

Autonomous Git & Figma PRs: When the system detects repeated team blindspots across 50+ posts, it autonomously creates GitHub Pull Requests updating brand-guidelines.md and syncs new design tokens directly into Figma. The brand handbook continuously heals and updates itself.

IMPROVEMENT 2 10-Persona Jury

Synthetic Audience Simulation: Expands the 2-agent debate into an autonomous panel of 10 synthetic AI personas (FTC auditor, skeptical enterprise buyer, competitor PR lead, tech critic) to predict virality, controversy, and sentiment with statistical confidence bounds before ad spend.

IMPROVEMENT 3 ZK Brand Proofs

Zero-Knowledge Publishing Mesh: Issues cryptographic proofs allowing enterprises to verify compliance with external agencies and ad platforms without exposing confidential embargoed launch data, with direct automated publishing to CMS and social networks.
