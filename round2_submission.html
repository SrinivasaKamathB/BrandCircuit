4 Explanation of Changes Made to Original Plan

During Round 1, our conceptual proposal focused primarily on static text highlighting and standard guideline lookup. In building the Round 2 MVP, we made three deliberate design enhancements to maximize real enterprise value:

  • From Passive Warnings to Consequence Simulation: Traditional checkers output dry error codes. We built the PR Blast Radius & Crisis Simulator to visualize the concrete cost of failure (viral backlash and FTC regulatory exposure), giving non-technical executives an immediate business case.
  • From Rigid LLM Rewrites to Multi-Agent Negotiation: Copywriters frequently reject AI-generated rewrites because they remove creative punch. We introduced the Maverick vs. Guardian Debate with a live compromise slider, allowing creators to balance legal safety with marketing conversion hooks.
  • From Simple Status Toggles to Cryptographic Passports: Replaced standard "Approved" boolean flags with verifiable SHA-256 digital passports to prevent post-approval content tampering in enterprise publishing pipelines.

5 Three Major Improvements for Round 3 (The Mastermind Roadmap)

As mandated by the competition rules, these three major additions represent meaningful architectural capabilities:

IMPROVEMENT 1 Self-Healing Playbook

Auto-PR to Git & Figma: When the system detects repeated team blindspots across 50+ posts, it autonomously drafts GitHub Pull Requests to update brand-guidelines.md and pushes design token patches directly into Figma.

IMPROVEMENT 2 10-Persona Jury

Synthetic Audience Simulation: Expands the 2-agent debate into an autonomous panel of 10 synthetic AI personas (FTC auditor, skeptical enterprise buyer, competitor PR lead, tech critic) to predict virality, controversy, and sentiment before ad spend.

IMPROVEMENT 3 ZK Brand Proofs

Zero-Knowledge Publishing Mesh: Issues cryptographic proofs allowing enterprises to verify compliance with external agencies and ad platforms without exposing confidential embargoed launch data, with direct automated publishing to CMS and social networks.
